Step 1 of 6

Your options

What you can do.

None of it is required.

01

Say what you think

One click per row, only where you disagree

Only for the handful you would do differently.

Includes
  • Take it off the shared list
  • Keep it, but only under a condition you write down
  • Flag that you have not made up your mind
  • Say where you test it, if you want to
02

Add what is missing

Straight into the section it belongs in

Goes on as a proposal, so the group sees it.

Includes
  • Propose a structure, with your reason
  • Record what the faculty decided, in one line
  • Leave a comment under your name
  • Comment on a whole block at once
03

Keep your own version

Yours alone, nobody else sees it

Switch things on or off for your section. The shared list does not move.

Includes
  • Add something the group left off
  • Drop something you do not cover
  • Or follow the department, row by row
  • Print or post your own version

Before you start

Three things worth knowing.

01

You are not signing up to teach less

The minimum we all cover. What you add on top is yours, same as always.

02

You cannot break anything

Nothing is deleted. It saves as you go. Stop any time. Changed your mind? Click again.

03

Nobody can write over your answers

Your marks are yours. Two of us can work on the same section at once.
