Step 1 of 6

What you can do

All of it is optional except reading. If a whole unit looks right to you, tick your way through it and you are done.

01

Cut something

Mark a structure to come off the shared minimum. It stays on the record and stays available to you in your own section.

02

Add something

Propose a structure straight into the section where it belongs. It lands marked Recommend Add so the group can talk about it.

03

Keep it with a condition

For the ones that stay only on the model, or that students name rather than identify. Write the condition in the reason box.

04

Record a decision

One line per structure saying where it landed. That is the line that prints beside the structure on the final handout.

05

Talk it through

Comments sit under your name and nothing overwrites them. Use them for the reasoning, and the decision line for the outcome.

06

Build your own list

Switch structures on or off for your own course, then print a handout that is yours. It changes nothing for anybody else.

What comes out of it

The marking is not the point. These are, and all three are built from the same records, so none of it is retyped and none of it can drift out of step with the review.

01

A web page

02

Every structure with its vote counts, the agreement behind it and what the rule recommends. Build it for the department list or for your own course.

04

A meeting agenda

Only the structures the rule could not settle, most-marked first. A department meeting can start from the real disagreements instead of reading a thousand rows aloud.

05

A lab planning view

Which structures are tested on the cadaver, the models, the slides, the bone box. Useful well beyond this review when you are deciding what to set out.

06

Everything above, filtered to what you switched on for your own section, with your name on it rather than the department's.

Yours alone

Three things worth knowing

This is the minimum, not the maximum

It is the shared floor the department writes exams from, so any exam in any section can draw on anything that ends up here. Everyone keeps the freedom to go further.

Leaving a row alone keeps it

You never have to click a thousand rows to agree with them. The only clicks that matter are the ones where you want something different.

Nothing you do overwrites anybody

Every mark is filed separately under your name. Two people can work on the same section at the same time without stepping on each other.

Open the review tool

Opens in a new tab. Put your name in and start anywhere.
