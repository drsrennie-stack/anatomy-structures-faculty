Faculty collaboration workspace

One shared list of every structure we currently test in lab, worked on by the whole anatomy faculty. Everything on here stays unless somebody marks it, so you only click the rows you want to change. Everything you do mark is filed under your name and sits alongside everyone else's, so nothing you do replaces anybody else's work.

What this list is, and how marking works

What this list is

This is the minimum, not the maximum. It is the shared floor the department writes exams from, so any exam in any section can draw on anything that ends up here.

It is not a ceiling on your teaching. Every one of us keeps the freedom to go further. If a structure does not make the minimum list, you can still put it on your own list and test your own students on it.

So marking something Recommend Remove is about what all of us must cover. It is not a vote to stop anyone covering it.

The dashed Keep circle on a row means that is where the row currently stands. Mark Cut if it should come off, Cond. if it stays only under certain circumstances, Unsure if you want to think about it, and Keep only when you want to push back on somebody else who flagged it.

At the end of every section there is a box to record that you have been through that block. It is how you keep track of what you have already covered, and how everyone else sees who has read what.
